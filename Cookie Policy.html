1. About Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a browser or device. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags and software development kits (SDKs).

2. Technologies on the UGS Website

Our website is intended to provide company information and links to our policies and games. The hosting provider may use essential technical mechanisms for security, traffic delivery and reliable operation. We do not intentionally use this statement to claim that the website is entirely cookie-free. The technologies actually used can depend on hosting configuration and any third-party features embedded on a page.

3. Technologies in Mobile Games

Mobile games may use advertising, analytics, crash reporting, purchase validation and platform SDKs. These technologies are not necessarily browser cookies and may process device identifiers or app events. Refer to the relevant game’s Privacy Policy and platform privacy controls.

4. Your Choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Some website features may not work as intended if essential cookies are disabled. You can manage mobile advertising choices through your device and app settings.

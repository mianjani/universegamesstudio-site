Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information about UGS and our games.

What is UGS?

UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED is an independent game development and publishing company based in Lahore, Pakistan.

Where can I find UGS games?

Our games may be distributed through third-party app stores. Availability depends on the game, device and region. Please use the official store listing for the relevant title.

How do I get technical support?

Email info@universegamesstudio.com and include the game title, device model, operating system version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

How do I make a privacy request?

Read our Privacy Policy and email info@universegamesstudio.com with the details of your request and the relevant game/platform.

How do I report copyright or other rights concerns?

Use the instructions on our Content & Takedown Policy page.

Does every UGS game use the same ads or analytics providers?

No. SDKs and service providers may vary by game, platform, territory and version. Check the applicable game disclosures.

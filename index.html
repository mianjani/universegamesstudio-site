Universe Games Studio (UGS) (SMC-Private) Limited is an independent game developer and publisher incorporated in Pakistan. We build our games in the Unity Engine and publish them on Android app stores.

Our games

All games are free to play and supported by ads. Some include optional in-app purchases.

Arrow Out: Block Tap Away

com.gs.ugs.tapout.arrowgame (Samsung)
com.amz.ugs.tapout.arrowgame (Amazon)

A 3D puzzle game. Tap each arrow block in the right order so it can slide away without hitting another block.

Available on Samsung Galaxy Store, Amazon Appstore and Huawei AppGallery.

Animal Kingdom: Merge Animal

A merge game. Combine matching animals to unlock new creatures and grow your kingdom.

Available on Samsung Galaxy Store.

Snake Escape: Slither Maze

com.gs.ugs.snakeout.games

A 3D maze puzzle. Guide each snake out of a tangled board without blocking the others.

Available on Samsung Galaxy Store.

Privacy Policy

Effective March 23, 2026. Last updated November 21, 2026.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Universe Games Studio (UGS) (SMC-Private) Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you play our games: Arrow Out: Block Tap Away, Animal Kingdom Merge Animal and Snake Escape: Slither Maze (the “Games”). By installing or playing a Game, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

The Games and the third-party services inside them may automatically collect:

  • Device model and operating system version.
  • IP address, used to estimate your country.
  • Device language and time zone.
  • Advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID or Samsung advertising ID.
  • Gameplay data, such as levels reached, scores and in-game events.
  • Crash and performance reports.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, phone number or precise location.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the app store you use (for example Samsung Galaxy Store or Amazon Appstore). We never see or store your card or bank details. We only receive confirmation of the item bought, the time and your country.

4. Advertising

The Games show third-party ads. These partners may use your advertising ID and IP address to show ads and to prevent fraud. Each partner has its own privacy policy:

  • InMobi
  • Unity Ads
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Mintegral
  • Google AdMob
  • TopOn (ad mediation)

You can limit ad personalization on Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (menu names vary by device).

5. How we use information

  • To run the Games and keep them stable.
  • To save your progress on your device.
  • To find and fix bugs.
  • To show ads and measure how they perform.
  • To confirm in-app purchases.

6. Sharing

We share information only with our advertising and analytics partners listed above, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Children

The Games are for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and transfers

We keep information only as long as needed to run the Games, meet legal duties or settle disputes. Our partners may process data on servers outside your country.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email info@universegamesstudio.com with the subject “Data request”.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

11. Changes

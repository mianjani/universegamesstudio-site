Content & Takedown Policy

How to report copyright or other rights concerns.

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Our Approach

UGS respects the intellectual property and other rights of others and expects the same from users of our Services. We review good-faith reports and take appropriate action where required.

2. How to Submit a Report

Email info@universegamesstudio.com with the subject “Takedown Request” and include:

  • Your name, contact details and, if applicable, the rights owner you represent.
  • A description of the work or right you believe is affected.
  • The location (game title, platform or page URL) of the content you are reporting.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the rights owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your report is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights owner.

3. Our Review

We may request additional information, remove or restrict content, notify the relevant party or take other steps we consider appropriate. Where required, we may share the report details with the person affected. Knowingly false reports may have legal consequences.

4. Counter-Notices

If you believe content was removed in error, contact us with the details and the reasons you believe the removal was mistaken, and we will review your request.

5. Other Content Concerns

For concerns about privacy, safety or other unlawful content, contact the same email address and identify the relevant game or page.

About the company

UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED designs, builds, tests and publishes mobile games. We are incorporated in Lahore, Pakistan, and registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) as a single-member private limited company.

Every game is tested on a range of Android devices before release and updated after launch. We monetize through third-party advertising and optional in-app purchases, and we publish our authorized seller list openly at /app-ads.txt so advertisers can verify who may sell our ad space.

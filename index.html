Privacy Policy

Developer: Universe Games Studio (UGS) (SMC-Private) Limited
Effective date: March 23, 2026 · Last updated: March 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Universe Games Studio (UGS) (SMC-Private) Limited (“we”, “us” or “our”) manages your information when you use our mobile applications, including Animal Kingdom Merge Animal (the “Game”). By installing or using our Game, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Automatically collected information

When you use our Game, our systems may automatically capture:

  • Device hardware model and operating system (OS) version.
  • IP address (used to determine general country-level location).
  • Device language settings and time zone.
  • Unique advertising identifiers (for example, Google Advertising ID or Samsung ID).
  • Gameplay metrics, including scores, levels reached and in-game events.
  • Diagnostic crash reports to identify technical failures.

2.2 In-app purchases

All financial transactions are processed securely through the Samsung Galaxy Store (or the respective platform store). We do not store or have access to your credit card or banking details. We only receive basic confirmation data, such as:

  • The item purchased.
  • The date and time of the transaction.
  • The user’s country.

2.3 Customer support

If you contact us for help, we may collect:

  • Your email address.
  • The details and history of your request.

3. Advertising & mediation

To keep our games free to play, we display third-party advertisements. Our Game uses the following platforms:

  • Unity Ads
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Google AdMob
  • Mintegral

These partners may use your Advertising ID and IP address to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraudulent activity. Each partner operates under its own independent privacy policy.

4. How we use your data

  • To make sure the Game works correctly on your device.
  • To synchronize and save your gameplay progress across sessions.
  • To troubleshoot bugs and improve overall stability.
  • To deliver and optimize advertising content.
  • To verify and process in-app purchases.

5. Children’s privacy

Our Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Data sharing

We share information only with:

  • Authorized advertising partners.
  • Performance and analytics service providers.
  • Legal authorities, if required by law or official regulations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for monetary gain.

7. Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as necessary to provide the Game services, comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your data.

To exercise these rights, email info@universegamesstudio.com.

9. Managing ads

You can control how ads are served to you. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID.

10. Security

We use industry-standard technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. International data transfers

Because our games are available globally, your data may be processed on cloud servers outside your home country. We make sure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your privacy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be shown by an updated “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

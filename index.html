Privacy Policy

Developer: Universe Games Studio (UGS) (SMC-Private) Limited
Effective date: March 23, 2026 · Last updated: March 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Universe Games Studio (UGS) (SMC-Private) Limited (“we”, “us” or “our”) manages your information across our mobile games, including Arrow Out: Block Tap Away and Animal Kingdom Merge Animal. By installing or playing our games, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you launch and interact with our games, our systems and SDKs may collect:

  • Device hardware model, processor specifications, and operating system (OS) version.
  • Coarse geographic location derived from anonymized IP address.
  • Device language settings, orientation, and local time zone.
  • Unique advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Samsung GAID).
  • Gameplay metrics (levels completed, scores, session durations, and in-game events).
  • Technical crash logs and diagnostic crash reports to identify stability faults.

2.2 In-App Purchases (IAP)

Financial transactions are handled exclusively by verified storefront billing systems (Samsung Galaxy Store, Amazon Appstore). We never store or access your credit cards, banking credentials, or payment passwords. We receive only basic confirmation receipt tokens.

2.3 Customer Support & Direct Communication

If you email us for game support or feedback, we collect your email address and message contents solely to resolve your inquiry.

3. Advertising Partners & Mediation

To provide games free of charge, our titles integrate third-party ad networks and mediation platforms compliant with IAB and COPPA standards:

  • InMobi: InMobi Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Unity Privacy Policy
  • Mintegral: Mintegral Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin MAX / TopOn Mediation: AppLovin Legal

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver stable gameplay, maintain high frame-rates, and fix software defects.
  • To save and restore player progression and high scores across play sessions.
  • To display frequency-capped, non-fraudulent advertising.
  • To validate legitimate digital goods purchased via platform storefronts.

5. Children’s Privacy Protection

Our games are intended for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal identity data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal details without parental consent, email us immediately for account deletion.

6. Your Rights & Ad Choice Settings

You can opt out of personalized interest-based ads via your Android device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

To request access, correction, or permanent removal of any gameplay records, please write to info@universegamesstudio.com.

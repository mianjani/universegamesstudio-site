Content & Takedown Policy

How to report content that may infringe rights or violate applicable law.

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Our Approach

UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED respects intellectual property rights and expects users and partners to do the same. We review credible reports concerning content distributed through our official website or games.

2. Submit a Report

If you believe content associated with UGS infringes your copyright, trademark, privacy or other legal rights, email info@universegamesstudio.com with the subject line “Content Takedown Request”. Include:

  • Your full name and contact email, and the organization you represent (if applicable).
  • A clear description of the content and where it appears, including the URL, game title, store listing or screenshots where possible.
  • A description of the right you believe has been infringed and the basis for your claim.
  • Evidence that you own the right or are authorized to act for its owner.
  • The action you are requesting and a statement that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

3. Review and Action

We may request additional information, review the report, restrict access to disputed material where appropriate, or refer the issue to the relevant platform or service provider. Submitting a report does not guarantee removal. We may reject incomplete, abusive or clearly unsupported notices.

4. Counter-Notice or Response

If you believe material was restricted in error, contact us with the relevant URL or game details, an explanation and supporting information. We will review the response where appropriate and subject to applicable law and platform procedures.

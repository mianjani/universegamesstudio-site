About UGS

Independent game development and publishing from Lahore, Pakistan.

Overview

UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED is an independent game development and publishing firm incorporated in Lahore, Pakistan. We work in the digital entertainment industry with a focus on creating and publishing mobile games and interactive software.

Our Core Expertise

Driven by a passion for innovation and storytelling, our studio works across the lifecycle of mobile applications and interactive software.

Game Design & Prototyping

Transforming original concepts into engaging, playable digital experiences through planning, prototyping and iteration.

Engine Excellence

Using the Unity Engine to develop interactive products with attention to performance, stability and cross-platform delivery.

Product Lifecycle Management

Working from concept and production through quality assurance, release preparation, publishing and post-launch optimization.

Our Vision & Commitment

At UGS, we believe games are digital universes. Our mission is to bring technology and entertainment together through accessible, enjoyable and thoughtfully developed experiences. Projects are reviewed with a focus on functionality, performance and player experience.

Corporate Identity

We aim to expand our presence in the global mobile gaming ecosystem through responsible publishing, strategic partnerships and innovative software solutions. We are committed to clear communication and professional integrity.

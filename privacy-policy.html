1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you play our mobile games or visit our website, https://universegamesstudio.com. It applies to these games (together, the “Games”):

  • Arrow Out: Block Tap Away (com.gs.ugs.tapout.arrowgame, com.amz.ugs.tapout.arrowgame)
  • Animal Kingdom: Merge Animal
  • Snake Escape: Slither Maze (com.gs.ugs.snakeout.games)

By installing or playing a Game you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the Game. This policy is meant to be read together with the privacy terms of the app store you download from and of the advertising partners listed in section 5.

4. How we use information and our legal bases

We use information to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the Games, and save your progress;
  • find and fix bugs, crashes and performance problems;
  • show advertising and measure how it performs, which is how the Games stay free;
  • process and verify in-app purchases and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • understand how players use the Games so we can improve them;
  • answer support requests and communicate about this policy;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a similar region, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the Game you chose to install); our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, stability, analytics and improving the Games); consent (where required, for example for personalized advertising or use of advertising identifiers); and legal obligation. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time through your device settings or by emailing us.

5. Advertising and our ad partners

The Games are free because they show ads. To do this we use third-party advertising and mediation services. Depending on the Game and version, these may include:

  • InMobi
  • Chartboost
  • LoopMe
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Mintegral
  • TopOn (ad mediation)

These partners may collect or receive your advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and information about ad views and clicks. They use it to deliver ads, measure results, prevent fraud and, unless you opt out, show interest-based ads. Each partner acts under its own privacy policy, linked above. We do not control how they process data.

Authorized sellers (app-ads.txt)

We publish a list of companies authorized to sell advertising in our Games at https://universegamesstudio.com/app-ads.txt, following the IAB Tech Lab standard. This helps advertisers confirm that ad space is genuine and protects players from fraudulent ads.

Your ad choices

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads) > Delete advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization. Menu names vary by device.
  • Samsung, Amazon Fire and Huawei devices: open your device’s Privacy or Advertising settings and limit ad tracking or reset the advertising ID.
  • Partner opt-outs: use the privacy links above to manage settings with each partner.

Turning off personalization does not remove ads. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Consent

Where the law requires your consent for advertising, you may see a consent choice when you open a Game. You can change your choice through the Game’s settings (if offered) or your device’s advertising settings.

6. Analytics and crash reporting

We may use analytics and crash-reporting tools to see which features are used, how long sessions last, and where crashes occur. These tools process device information, advertising or app-instance identifiers, and diagnostic data on our behalf, and may only use it to provide their services to us or as their own policies describe.

7. In-app purchases and app stores

Purchases are handled entirely by the app store you use (Samsung Galaxy Store, Amazon Appstore or Huawei AppGallery). We never receive or store your card, bank or billing details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, which item, when, and your country. The store’s own privacy policy governs the information it collects about you.

8. Children’s privacy

The Games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age set by your local law, such as 16 in parts of Europe). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We have set our advertising services accordingly and do not knowingly show interest-based ads to children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it promptly.

9. How we share information

We share information only with:

  • Advertising and mediation partners (section 5), to show and measure ads and prevent fraud;
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers (section 6) that work for us;
  • App stores, to process purchases and distribute the Games;
  • Professional advisers and authorities, where required by law, to protect our rights, or to prevent fraud or harm;
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganized, under the same protections.

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, under some laws (for example certain US state privacy laws), sharing advertising identifiers with ad partners can count as a “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. If those laws apply to you, you can opt out using the ad choices in section 5 or by emailing us.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including targeted advertising;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • not be treated unfairly for using your rights;
  • complain to your local data-protection authority.

To use these rights, email info@universegamesstudio.com with the subject “Data request”. Tell us the Game name, your device type and, if you want us to find your data, your advertising ID. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We respond within the time required by law, generally 30 to 45 days.

13. Deleting your data

Because the Games have no accounts, the data we hold is linked to your device or advertising ID, not to your name.
  1. Email info@universegamesstudio.com with the subject “Data request – delete” and include the Game name and your advertising ID (found in your device’s Google, Amazon or Huawei ads settings).
  2. We delete or anonymize the data we hold about that identifier and tell you when it is done.
  3. You can also stop all further collection by uninstalling the Game and resetting or deleting your advertising ID.

Data held by advertising partners must be managed with those partners using the links in section 5.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) where available and limited access to our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Our website

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. Our website may link to app stores and partner sites that have their own privacy policies.

16. Do Not Track

The Games and website do not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals because no standard exists. Use the device-level ad choices in section 5 instead.

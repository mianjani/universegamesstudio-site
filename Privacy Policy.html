Privacy Policy

How UGS handles information in its games, applications and website.

Effective Date: March 23, 2026 · Last Updated: November 21, 2026

Developer: UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how UNIVERSE GAMES STUDIO(UGS) (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED (“UGS”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our mobile games, applications and website (collectively, the “Services”). Some games may have additional or game-specific privacy notices. Where a game-specific notice applies, read it together with this policy. By using the Services, you acknowledge this policy, subject to any consent choices required by applicable law.

2. Information We May Collect

2.1 Device and technical information

Depending on the game, platform and SDK integrations, we or our service providers may receive device model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, IP address, approximate country or region, network information, and device or advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID or a platform-provided advertising identifier.

2.2 Gameplay and diagnostics

We may process gameplay information such as game progress, scores, levels, session events, settings and feature interactions. Crash logs, performance diagnostics and error reports may be collected to identify bugs, improve stability and maintain the Services.

2.3 Advertising information

Where advertising is enabled, advertising partners and mediation services may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, ad impressions, clicks, approximate location and interaction signals to serve, measure, limit or prevent fraudulent advertising. Availability and processing depend on the particular game, region, consent status and SDK version.

2.4 Purchases

In-app purchases, where offered, are processed by the relevant platform store (for example, Samsung Galaxy Store, Google Play or another supported store). We do not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. We may receive limited transaction information such as product purchased, transaction or receipt identifier, purchase status, date and relevant country/region to validate purchases, provide entitlements and handle support.

2.5 Support communications

If you contact us, we may process your email address, the information you include in your message, relevant game/device details and the history of the support request.

2.6 Website information

When you visit our website, our hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address, requested page, browser information, timestamp and security events, for delivery, reliability and protection of the website.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some games may use third-party advertising or mediation services, depending on the game and version. These may include Unity Ads, AppLovin MAX, Google AdMob and Mintegral. The relevant provider may collect or receive device and advertising information to deliver ads, measure performance, manage frequency, detect invalid traffic and comply with legal obligations. Not every provider is necessarily used in every game.

Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review the privacy information presented by the relevant provider and in the applicable app-store listing. We do not control independent third-party privacy practices.

4. How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain and deliver game features and services.
  • Save or restore gameplay progress and validate in-app purchases where supported.
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, improve performance and develop new features.
  • Display, measure and optimize advertisements where enabled and permitted.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service information.
  • Protect the Services, detect fraud or abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

5. Legal Bases and Consent

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis, processing may rely on performance of a service you request, our legitimate interests in securing and improving the Services, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent. Where consent is required for personalized advertising or certain device access, it should be obtained through the relevant platform or consent mechanism. You may change available advertising or privacy choices through your device or app settings.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with: (a) advertising and mediation partners used by a particular game; (b) analytics, crash-reporting, hosting and technical service providers; (c) platform stores for purchase validation and app distribution; and (d) authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety and security. Service providers are expected to use information for the services they provide and in accordance with applicable requirements.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Some privacy laws define “sale”, “sharing” or targeted advertising broadly; where applicable, you may exercise the choices described in this policy.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not specifically directed at children under 13, unless a particular game listing says otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps. Age ratings, audience settings and child-directed status may differ by game and store.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the Services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods vary by data type, game, service provider and legal obligation. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted, anonymized or securely disposed of where reasonably practicable.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To submit a request, email info@universegamesstudio.com with the game name, platform, country/region and a description of your request. We may need to verify your request before responding.

10. Advertising Controls

You can manage advertising personalization using the privacy or advertising controls provided by your device, operating system, app store or the relevant game. Android menu names and paths vary by device and OS version; look under Settings for Google/Privacy/Ads or the applicable Advertising ID controls. Resetting or deleting an advertising identifier may affect ad personalization but does not necessarily stop all advertising.

11. International Data Transfers

Our Services may be used internationally. We and our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for cross-border transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential technologies provided by the hosting platform for security, delivery and basic functionality. If analytics, embedded media or other non-essential technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent mechanism will be updated. Mobile games may use SDK technologies that are different from website cookies. See our Cookie Statement.

14. Third-Party Links and Platforms

Our games and website may link to app stores, social networks, partner services or third-party websites. Those services are operated independently and have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies before providing information.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our Services, technology, legal requirements or business practices. The updated date will appear at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the relevant game or website where appropriate.
